id: mono-accent-eyebrow
title: No mono accent eyebrow above a heading
---

## Rule

Do not open a section, hero, or page with the eyebrow stack: a small monospaced, accent-colored, often uppercase or tracked-out label, then a larger heading beneath it, then a paragraph of supporting description. The ban covers the whole three-part pattern and the mono accent eyebrow on its own, however it is labelled — kicker, overline, tagline, section tag.

## Instead

Lead with the heading itself and let the description follow. When the section genuinely needs a category marker, carry it in navigation, a breadcrumb, a tab, or a badge in the project's body font — not as a decorative line of mono accent text stacked above the title.
