---
id: letters-as-icons
title: No letters used as icons
---

## Rule

Do not build an icon out of a letter, initial, or monogram: a character in a circle or rounded square standing in for an avatar, a logo, a brand mark, a category glyph, or a list bullet.

## Instead

Use a real icon from `ui/icons/*` or the target project's existing icon set, an actual image or logo asset when one exists, or a plain text label when no icon is warranted.
